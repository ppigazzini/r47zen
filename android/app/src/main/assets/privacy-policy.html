Privacy Policy

This privacy policy covers the Android app R47 Zen RPN Calculator, shown on the home screen as "R47 Zen", package io.github.ppigazzini.r47zen. It is the same app whether you install it from Google Play or from the GitHub releases of its source repository.

Last updated: 2026-10-14

Summary

R47 Zen collects no personal data. It has no accounts, ads, analytics, or crash reporting, and it does not request the Android Internet permission, so it has no way to send anything off your device. What it stores stays on your device, in your own Android backup, or in a folder you choose.

Who We Are And How To Contact Us

R47 Zen is developed and published by Pasquale Pigazzini, an individual developer, as an independent Android build of the open-source C47 calculator core.

  • Source code: github.com/ppigazzini/r47zen
  • Privacy questions and requests: open an issue at github.com/ppigazzini/r47zen/issues. Issues are public. To ask privately, write to the developer email address shown on the app's Google Play listing.

Data The App Handles On Your Device

The app handles the following data only on your device, to work as a calculator:

  • Calculator state and settings. The stack, registers, programs, and other calculator memory, plus your preferences (keypad layout, display colors, haptics, and similar). They are kept in Android app-private storage so the app can restore your last session.
  • Files you open or save. Through Android's system file picker you can open and save calculator files (STATE, PROGRAMS, SAVFILES) and screenshots of the calculator display (SCREENS). If you choose a Work Directory, the app can read and write files in that one folder only.
  • Clipboard. The Copy actions put the X register, the stack registers, or all registers on the Android clipboard, only when you choose them. Paste Number reads the clipboard once, only when you choose it, and types the number characters it finds into the calculator; it ignores the rest and keeps no copy. On Android 13 and later the system shows a notice when the app reads the clipboard.
  • Vibration. The app asks Android for the vibration permission, for haptic key feedback only.

The app requests no Internet, location, camera, microphone, contacts, phone, SMS, or notification permission, and no broad access to your storage.

Data The App Does Not Collect

  • No data is sent to the developer or to anyone else. Without the Internet permission, the app cannot connect to a network.
  • No user account or sign-in.
  • No advertising or advertising identifier.
  • No analytics, tracking, or crash-reporting tools, and no third-party software that collects data.
  • Links in the app, such as View Android Source, open in your web browser, which is a separate app with its own privacy policy.

How Data Is Used

The data described above is used only on your device, to run the calculator, restore your session, apply your settings, and open or save the files you ask for. It is never used for advertising, profiling, or tracking.

Sharing

The developer does not sell, rent, or share your data, and receives none from the app. Data leaves the app only when you choose to move it:

  • Files you save to a folder that belongs to a cloud provider, such as Google Drive, are stored by that provider under its own privacy policy.
  • Text you copy to the clipboard can be read by the apps you paste it into.
  • Android backup and device transfer are described under Backup And Device Transfer.

Google Play, GitHub, And Android

Google Play and GitHub, which deliver the app, process data under their own privacy policies when you download and update it. If you allowed your device to share usage and diagnostics data with Google, Google Play may give the developer aggregate install statistics and crash and freeze reports (app version, device model, Android version, and the technical trace of the failure). These reports come from Google Play, not from code in the app. The developer uses them only to find and fix bugs.

Retention And Deletion

  • App-private data stays on your device until you use Reset to Factory Defaults in Settings, clear the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app. Each of these deletes it.
  • Reset to Factory Defaults does not delete files you saved in a Work Directory or elsewhere with the file picker. They stay until you delete them with a file manager or with the provider that holds them.
  • Use Android Default Folder in Settings makes the app forget the saved Work Directory and gives up its access to that folder.
  • Messages you send to the developer are kept only as long as needed to answer you. Ask, and a private message will be deleted.

Backup And Device Transfer

Android may copy the app's calculator state and settings to your Google account backup and to a new device during a device transfer. These copies are managed by Android and Google, and the developer cannot read them. The app leaves its Work Directory choice and its saved-slot settings out of backup and transfer. On Android 12 and later, the app allows a cloud backup only when your device can encrypt it end to end, which needs a screen lock. You can turn backup off in your device's Android settings.

Security

  • App-private storage is protected by Android's app sandbox: other apps cannot read it.
  • The app has no Internet permission, so a fault in the app cannot leak your data over a network.
  • Access outside app-private storage goes through the Android system file picker and is limited to the files and the folder you choose.
  • The source code is public, so anyone can check these statements. The README in the source repository lists the SHA-256 fingerprint of the certificate that signs the official release, so you can check that your copy is genuine.

Children

R47 Zen is made for everyone aged 6 and over, children under 13 included. It collects no personal information from anyone, of any age: no names, contact details, photos, voice, location, device identifiers, or usage data. It has no ads, no in-app purchases, no chat or social features, and no accounts. Because nothing is collected, the app never needs a parent's consent under the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), and there is no child data to review or delete.

Parents and guardians can ask questions at the developer email address shown on the app's Google Play listing.

Your Rights

Depending on where you live, laws such as the EU and UK GDPR or US state privacy laws give you rights to access, correct, or delete personal data that a developer holds about you. The developer holds none from the app. If you contacted the developer, you can exercise these rights over that message through the contact options above. The developer does not sell or share personal information as those laws define it.

Google Play Data Safety

The app's Google Play Data safety section declares that the app collects no data and shares no data. Google Play counts data as collected when an app sends it off your device. This app sends nothing: it processes data on your device and stores files only where you tell it to.

Changes To This Policy

When the app's handling of data changes, this policy is updated in the same release, here and in the copy inside the app (Settings, About R47 Zen, View Privacy Policy), and the Last updated date changes. Every past version is in the repository history.
