R47 Zen

The R47 calculator on Android, running the C47 core.

R47 Zen packages the upstream C47 calculator core as an Android app for the R47 calculator variant. It is independently maintained and is not an official release of the C47 project.

The Android app

  • Keys that follow the calculator. Each key shows the labels the core gives it in its current state: the f and g shifts, alpha letters, and USER mode, and the softkeys show the menu on the display. Settings chooses how much follows: Dynamic Full UI, Dynamic Alpha, Dynamic USER f/g, Fixed, or Zen.
  • Graphs you can touch. Drag and pinch a graph on the display to pan and zoom it.
  • Your display. Four LCD themes, Gray Matrix, Green Vintage, Amber Quartz, and Blue Liquid, a negative LCD, full-screen mode, and picture in picture.
  • Copy and paste. Copy the X register, the stack, or every register, and paste a number from the clipboard.
  • Your files. Choose a work folder for portable STATE, PROGRAMS, SAVFILES, DATA, and SCREENS files; your last session comes back when the app restarts.
  • Feel and sound. Haptic feedback at the system or your own duration, and a simulated beeper.
  • The menu marker. The orange and blue marker at the top right opens Settings, Copy, Paste Number, and Picture in Picture.

Get the app

  • Download the APK from the latest release and install it.
  • Verify a downloaded APK against the release certificate.
  • Update with Obtainium to keep it current.

Owner's manual

The owner's manual (PDF) is the cropped manual of the upstream C47 project, built from its latest sources. The app's Settings opens the same file.

Privacy

R47 Zen collects no personal data and does not request the Android Internet permission. The privacy policy says what it stores on your device.

Support

  • A bug or a question about the Android app: open an issue.
  • A calculator behavior that the desktop C47 simulator shows too belongs to the core: report it to the upstream C47 project.
  • A security problem: follow the security policy, never a public issue.

Source and license

R47 Zen is free software under the GNU General Public License, version 3. Its source is on GitHub, and every release there attaches the source of the calculator core its APK is built from.
